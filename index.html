Enkang Maasai · homestead
Sidai beautiful · as it should be

Enkang means homestead in Maasai, the heart of community. Sidai means beautiful. Together they describe exactly what this place is.

Nestled in Ewuaso Kedong, the resort blends authentic Maasai heritage with modern comfort for guests who seek more than a bed, they seek a genuine encounter with land, culture, and stillness.

Nature

Open savannah, fresh air, and undisturbed ecosystems — the land here is alive in ways the city has forgotten.

Culture

Living Maasai traditions woven into every experience, from the welcome to the fire that burns through the night.

Rest

Designed to slow you down, no schedules, no rush. Time moves differently here, and that is entirely the point.

Hospitality

Every guest welcomed as family in the true spirit of Enkang, a homestead where no one is a stranger for long.

"Together, Enkang Sidai means exactly what you will find here, a beautiful homestead at the edge of the wild."
